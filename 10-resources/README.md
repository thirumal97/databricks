## Exam Preparation Tips

### Study Materials Priority
1. Official Databricks documentation (highest priority)
2. Databricks Academy courses
3. Hands-on practice in Databricks workspace
4. Community tutorials and examples
5. Books and video courses

### Practice Strategy
- Spend 60% time on hands-on practice
- Spend 30% time on documentation/courses
- Spend 10% time on mock exams
